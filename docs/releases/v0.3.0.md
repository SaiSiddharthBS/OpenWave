## ✨ What's new

### 🤖 OpenWave for Android
- **The full app runs on your phone**: search, songs-only playback, gapless autoplay, profiles, playlists, Spotify imports, insights, lyrics and **offline downloads**. No computer or account needed.
- **Real phone controls**: a media notification, lock-screen controls, and headphone and Bluetooth buttons.
- **Plays nicely with your phone**: it pauses for calls and other apps, pauses when headphones are unplugged, and keeps playing when you switch apps or lock the screen.
- **Share to OpenWave**: share any YouTube or YouTube Music link to OpenWave and it plays the song. `openwave://` links work too.
- **One library everywhere**: in **Settings → Library**, keep your library on the phone, or connect to OpenWave on your computer and share the same likes and playlists.
- **Backups and insight cards** open Android's share sheet, so you can send them anywhere.

### 🔄 One-tap updates
From now on, OpenWave tells you when a new version is out and downloads the right file for your device in one tap, on Android, Windows, Linux and Mac. Your library stays as it is. You can also check any time in **Settings → About**.

### 💎 New Mac menu-bar icon
The menu-bar mini player now shows OpenWave's silver glass wave in full colour, instead of a flat silhouette.

### 🔐 Checksums for every download
Every installer now has a `.sha256` file next to it, plus a combined `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### 🔧 Fixes
- A song you downloaded from a music video now shows as downloaded and plays offline. Before, it tried to stream.
- Opening the Windows or Linux app a second time now opens another window instead of taking over the running engine.

Full details are in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/SaiSiddharthBS/OpenWave/blob/v0.3.0/CHANGELOG.md).
