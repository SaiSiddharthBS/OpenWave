You've been invited to listen and add songs together. Everyone in it can add, remove and reorder songs from their own OpenWave, and sees everyone else's changes.

Get OpenWave
  1. Copy this invite link (the button above).
  2. Open OpenWave and go to Library → Join.
  3. Paste the link and tap Join. The songs appear in a few seconds.

The playlist's key is in this link after the “#”. It never leaves your device: this page doesn't send it anywhere, and only people with the link can read the playlist. Only share it with people you trust.
